# Capstone Submission Instructions

Everything below is due on Demo Day.

## Deliverables

1. **Working prototype.** A repository we can run without help, with a reproducible environment (a
   container, or setup instructions with pinned dependencies). It includes your **eval set and results**.
2. **Product brief.** What you built, who it is for, how it works, and the design decisions you made,
   including an architecture diagram and the alternatives you rejected. Four pages maximum.
3. **Presentation deck.** The problem, the solution, the full product vision (what you would build with more
   time and resources), the demo narrative, and a technical diagram of what you built. The demo narrative
   follows a user story that shows the experience before and after your product.
4. **Demo video, 3 to 5 minutes.** A screencast, used if the live demo fails.
5. **Technical appendix.** Model choices, data sources, context and prompt strategy, and the
   engineering decisions you would defend in an interview.

## How to submit

Your GitHub repository is your submission. Push everything to it by Demo Day, and make sure the instructors
are collaborators.

- Put the product brief, the deck and the technical appendix in a `deliverables/` folder, as PDFs.
- Link the demo video from your README, and check that the instructors can open it.
